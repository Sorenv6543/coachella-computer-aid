# CCA Illustration Style Lock

The shared "one world" spec. Every one of the 300 illustrations is generated with
this style prefix so the whole set looks like a single, coherent library — the
prerequisite for being able to pick from a large group without it looking random.
Derived from the Brand Bible. Change it here, regenerate, and all prompts update.

## Style
Warm, friendly **flat vector illustration** — the "modern, approachable" feel of a
national-park visitor center crossed with a neighborhood community center. Rounded,
soft organic shapes. Gentle flat shading, subtle or no outlines, no hard corporate
edges. Warm golden-hour lighting throughout. Generous negative space, uncluttered.
Never photorealistic, never cold, never dark.

## Locked palette (use only these)
cream `#FDF6EC` · sand `#F8E9D5` · warm orange `#F4A259` · clay `#B95E23` ·
sky blue `#78B8D9` · sage green `#8BAE7B` · charcoal `#343434`.

## Setting
Coachella Valley: San Jacinto mountains, desert light, palms, saguaros — used where
a scene calls for place. Interiors are warm, sunlit, homey.

## Recurring characters (keep consistent across every image)
- **Robert** — 72, retired teacher, silver hair, blue cardigan, warm smile.
- **Maria** — 68, orange sweater, hair in a bun, houseplants nearby.
- **Helen** — 76, cheerful, light cardigan, surrounded by plants.
- **Carlos** — 60s veteran, relaxed, often a coffee mug and tablet.
- **Ana** — 28, the helper: backpack, laptop, confident and approachable.
- **David** — professional, uses a wheelchair, headset, works from home.

## Always avoid
text or lettering, watermarks, logos, photorealism, harsh/dark shadows, cold or
desaturated tones, cluttered busy backgrounds, off-palette colors.

## Per-category framing
- characters → square, single figure or pair, plain warm background, portrait feel
- hero → wide 16:9 cinematic scene, mountains/light, room for overlay text
- service / community / workshop → 4:3 scene, characters mid-activity
- device → square, single object, floating on cream, soft shadow
- background → wide, minimal, no characters, usable behind text
- marketing / ui → 4:3, clean composition with clear focal point
- icon → 1:1, single simple icon glyph only (isolated pictogram like an app icon) on a
  solid warm card background (cream, sand, or orange) that fills the frame edge-to-edge
  — never transparent, never a checkerboard pattern, never black, never pure white; no
  scene, landscape, room, background illustration, characters, or props beyond the
  symbol; thick rounded strokes, one or two palette colors
