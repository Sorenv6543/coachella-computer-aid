01 · Mission & Vision

Why we exist.

Our Mission

To empower people throughout the Coachella Valley by making technology accessible, understandable, and less intimidating — with patient, personalized support for seniors, people with disabilities, immigrant families, veterans, and anyone facing digital barriers.

Our Vision

A community where technology creates opportunities instead of obstacles, and every person — regardless of age, income, language, or ability — has the confidence to connect, learn, work, and thrive.

02 · The Why

The reason
underneath.

Technology has become essential for everyday life — staying connected with family, accessing healthcare, applying for jobs, managing money, and taking part in the community. Yet many people are left behind, because technology can feel overwhelming or out of reach.

We believe no one should be excluded simply because they need a patient teacher.

This page may never appear publicly — but it's the heart of everything. The work isn't repairing machines. It's teaching confidence: helping someone make their first video call, spot a scam before it costs them, or fill out a form that felt impossible an hour ago. Those are the moments this organization exists for.

06 · Brand Voice

Say it warmly.

Every sentence should lower a person's anxiety. We describe the outcome someone wants, never the technical process. When in doubt, write it the way you'd say it to a neighbor across the fence.

Never sayVirus removal
We sayWe'll help get your computer running smoothly again.
Never sayRemote desktop support
We sayWe can safely help you from home, if you'd like.
Never sayComputer diagnostics
We sayWe'll figure out what's going on together.
Never saySubmit / Error 404
We saySend → and plain, kind guidance when something goes wrong.

09 · Typography

Big, round,
readable.

Two rounded, humanist faces — chosen because they stay friendly at large sizes and legible for older eyes. Nunito carries headlines; Nunito Sans carries body. Both are free and open, so the whole ecosystem can use them at no cost.

Nunito · 900
Display
We'll figure it out together
Nunito · 800
Headings
Patient help, at your pace
Nunito Sans · 400
Body · 18px min
Technology should make life easier — not more confusing. We'll walk through it one step at a time, as many times as you need.
Nunito · 800
Labels / eyebrows
Who we help
Size floor. Body text never drops below 18px on screen (16px absolute minimum for fine print). Line length caps around 70 characters, with generous line-height (1.6+) so paragraphs never feel dense.

10 · Illustration Style

One warm world.

Always

  • Soft rounded edges
  • Flat shapes, gentle gradients
  • Warm natural lighting
  • Smiling, relaxed people
  • Only the six brand colors
  • Generous breathing room

Never

  • Dark corporate colors
  • Hard shadows or sharp edges
  • Angry or stressed expressions
  • Stock-photo realism
  • Tiny text or clutter
  • Cold, clinical framing

North star: Google Illustrations × Apple × Airbnb × a National Park visitor center. Every image should look like it came from the same hand.

12 · Accessibility

Built for
everyone.

Accessibility isn't a feature we add at the end — it's the baseline the whole system is measured against. Target: WCAG 2.1 AA and beyond.

Large text by default18px+ body, scalable without breaking layout.
AA contrast, verifiedCharcoal on cream; text-safe accent shades.
Full keyboard navigationVisible focus rings on every control.
Screen-reader readySemantic markup, alt text, ARIA labels.
Reduced-motion respectedAnimations soften or stop on request.
Bilingual from day oneEnglish and Spanish, equal footing.
Large touch targets44px minimum; easy to tap with less-steady hands.
Plain languageNo jargon; short sentences; clear next steps.
