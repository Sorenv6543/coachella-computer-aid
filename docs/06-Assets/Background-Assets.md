# Background Assets

**Illustrations and Visual Elements for Scene Settings**

---

## 🏔️ Core Background Elements

These recurring backgrounds create a sense of place and consistency throughout the site.

### San Jacinto Mountains
- **Primary hero background** — Mountain range visible behind scenes
- **Warm, inviting aesthetic** — Golden hour lighting, soft colors
- **Local relevance** — Immediately recognizable to Coachella Valley residents
- **Emotional connection** — Grounding, safe, familiar
- **Usage:** Homepage hero, service pages, hero headers
- **Color palette:** Blues (#78B8D9), oranges (#F4A259), warm tones
- **Variations:** Different times of day (sunrise, afternoon, sunset)

### Desert Landscaping
- **Native plants** — Palms, desert flowers, shrubs
- **Appropriate context** — Reflects actual environment
- **Warm, natural feeling** — Not harsh or stark
- **Part of identity** — "This is the Coachella Valley"
- **Usage:** Outdoor workshop scenes, community gathering spaces
- **Color palette:** Greens (#8BAE7B), earth tones, natural colors

### Bright Blue Sky
- **Optimistic, warm feeling** — Never dark or gloomy
- **Accessibility** — High contrast for readability
- **Consistent time of day** — Daylight, daytime scenarios
- **Usage:** All outdoor illustrations, outdoor workshop locations
- **Color palette:** Sky blue (#78B8D9), light and airy

## 🎨 Color & Mood Specifications

### Overall Approach
All backgrounds use only the [[../../01-Project-Foundation/Brand-Bible.md|brand color palette]]:
- Cream (#FDF6EC) — Backgrounds, space
- Sand (#F8E9D5) — Accent, warmth
- Orange (#F4A259) — Energy, warmth
- Sky Blue (#78B8D9) — Trust, calm
- Sage Green (#8BAE7B) — Nature, growth
- Charcoal (#343434) — Text, grounding

### Lighting
- **Warm, golden lighting** — Never harsh or cold
- **Natural daylight preferred** — Windows, outdoor light
- **Soft shadows** — Dimensional but not dark
- **No dark corners** — Welcoming, visible spaces
- **Consistent time of day** — Daylight scenarios (early morning to late afternoon)

### Atmosphere
- **Welcoming, never intimidating** — Open, friendly spaces
- **Organized but lived-in** — Comfortable, real environments
- **Accessible, inclusive design** — Clear navigation, visible features
- **Age-appropriate** — Designs that seniors find approachable
- **Technology integrated naturally** — Not futuristic, just everyday

---

## 🏗️ Technical Specifications

### Format
- **SVG preferred** — Scalable, flexible, small file size
- **WebP or PNG fallback** — High-quality raster backup
- **Responsive** — Works at multiple sizes

### Usage
- **Hero sections** — Full-width backgrounds (1920×1440px)
- **Content backgrounds** — Section backgrounds (can be patterns or illustrations)
- **Card backgrounds** — Subtle (low opacity, secondary color)
- **Illustration layers** — Characters placed over backgrounds

### Performance
- **Optimized for web** — < 200KB file size
- **Lazy loaded** — Load on demand
- **Responsive images** — Different sizes for different devices
