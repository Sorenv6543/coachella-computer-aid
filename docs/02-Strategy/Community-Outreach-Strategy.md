# Community Outreach Strategy

**How Coachella Computer Aid Reaches & Serves the Community**

---

## 🎯 Overview

This strategy outlines how we identify, reach, and serve the Coachella Valley community. Rather than waiting for people to come to us, we go to them through partnerships, workshops, and visible community presence.

---

## 👥 Who We Serve

### Seniors (60+)
**Needs:** Connection, safety, device basics  
**Topics:**
- Learning smartphones and tablets
- Video calls with family
- Telehealth appointments
- Email management
- Photo organization
- Scam protection and fraud awareness
- Smart TV setup and streaming services
- Password management
- Device safety and backups

**Motivations:**
- Stay connected with grandchildren
- Access healthcare digitally
- Gain independence

---

### People with Disabilities
**Needs:** Accessibility, adaptive technology, personalized support  
**Topics:**
- Adaptive technology setup
- Accessibility settings (text size, voice control, screen readers)
- Voice assistants and smart home devices
- Screen reader training
- Large text and high-contrast options
- Device setup and configuration
- Smart home technology
- Remote support options

**Motivations:**
- Independence and self-reliance
- Better access to services
- Meaningful digital participation

---

- School portal access and teacher communication

- Digital literacy in Spanish

**Motivations:**

- Economic opportunity

### Veterans

**Topics:**

### Low-Income Families

### Anyone Needing Technology Help
**Core Philosophy:** Technology shouldn't require expertise. Everyone deserves patient help.

If you're confused by technology, regardless of age, background, or circumstance, we're here for you.

## 🏫 Workshops & Group Training

Rather than serving only individuals, we teach groups. This scales impact and builds community.

### Senior Tech Mondays
- **Location:** Senior centers, libraries, churches, retirement communities
- **Format:** Monthly 1-2 hour sessions
- **Focus:** Current topics selected by attendees
- **Cost:** Free or pay-what-you-can

### Scam Awareness Workshops
- Recognizing phishing emails
- Phone scam awareness
- AI-generated scam alerts
- Fake email identification
- Facebook scams
- Banking and financial fraud

### Smartphone Basics
- Photo organization and sharing
- Contact management
- Text messaging and calling
- Video call setup (FaceTime, WhatsApp, Zoom)
- Phone settings and accessibility
- Finding and using apps

### Computer Basics
- Mouse and keyboard usage
- Printing and troubleshooting
- Email fundamentals
- Downloading and installing programs
- Internet browsing
- File organization

### Technology for Caregivers
- Supporting aging parents remotely
- Remote device support and monitoring
- Password management systems
- Medical technology and apps
- Emergency contact systems
- Independence while staying connected

### Spanish Technology Workshops
- Entirely bilingual delivery
- Focus on immigrant families
- Government services navigation (taxes, benefits, visas)
- School system portals and communication
- Healthcare portal navigation
- Job search and employment technology

## 🤝 Community Outreach Channels

### Facebook Groups
- **Strategy:** Weekly helpful tips, no selling
- **Goal:** Become known as "the local tech person"
- **Content:** Tips, advice, Q&A, community building
- **Engagement:** Comments, shares, real conversations

### Libraries
- **Service:** Free classes and workshops
- **Frequency:** Monthly or bi-weekly sessions
- **Format:** Structured workshops or drop-in office hours
- **Audience:** General public, seniors, job seekers
- **Benefit:** Neutral, trusted community space

### Senior Centers
- **Service:** Monthly presentations and office hours
- **Topics:** Device help, scam awareness, health tech
- **Activities:** Tablet troubleshooting, phone setup help, 1-on-1 consultations
- **Frequency:** Weekly or bi-weekly presence

### Nursing Homes & Retirement Communities
- **Service:** "Technology Connection Days"
- **Goal:** Help residents stay in touch with family
- **Activities:** Video call setup, tablet training, photo sharing
- **Impact:** Combat isolation, maintain family connections

### Churches
- **Service:** Technology office hours
- **Format:** Drop-in help sessions
- **Community:** After-service tech help, community building
- **Partnership:** Volunteer opportunities, workshop space

### Schools
- **Service:** Family engagement nights
- **Topics:** Parent portal navigation, teacher communication
- **Audience:** Grandparents, immigrant families, older parents
- **Goal:** Bridge technology gap in school systems

### Homeless Organizations & Job Training Centers
- **Service:** Practical technology support for employment
- **Help:** Email account recovery, resume building, job portal navigation
- **Topics:** Government website access, ID replacement, digital literacy
- **Impact:** Economic empowerment through technology

### Hospitals & Medical Practices
- **Service:** Telehealth setup and training
- **Topics:** Patient portal navigation, appointment scheduling
- **Audience:** Seniors, people with chronic conditions
- **Partnership:** Healthcare providers seeking tech support

### What We Create
- Video tutorials for common tasks
- Printable guides and handouts

- Step-by-step photo guides

### Where We Share

### Goals

- Build trust: Real people, real solutions, no jargon
- Scale impact: More people helped through content than individual appointments
- Community: Build a culture where tech help is normal and accessible

## 💰 Business Model Integration

Our outreach strategy supports a **Pay-What-You-Can** model:

- **Workshops:** Free or suggested donation
- **One-on-one help:** $0-$75 sliding scale
- **Remote support:** Affordable, accessible pricing
- **Group training:** Free for organizations (they cover costs or donations)

This ensures we serve regardless of income while maintaining sustainability.

---

## 🌍 Long-Term Vision

By consistently showing up in the community with genuine help (no selling), we become known as:

- "The friendly tech people"
- "The ones who explain things clearly"
- "Who actually care about helping"
- "Local, trustworthy, patient"

This reputation becomes our most powerful marketing tool.
