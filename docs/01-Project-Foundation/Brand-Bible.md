## 🎯 Mission

To empower every member of the Coachella Valley with the knowledge, confidence, and access to use technology safely and independently, regardless of age, disability, language, or income.

*Instead of listing demographics, we present them as people with stories.*

### We Serve

**Seniors**
Helping them stay connected with family, avoid scams, and use technology confidently.

**People with Disabilities**
Making technology more accessible through adaptive tools, accessibility features, and personalized support.

**Spanish-speaking Families**
Offering bilingual assistance with devices, online services, school portals, healthcare websites, and government resources.

**Veterans**
Helping navigate VA portals, telehealth appointments, and online benefits.

**Low-Income Families**
Providing affordable technology support and connecting people with low-cost internet and device programs whenever possible.

**Anyone Feeling Overwhelmed by Technology**
Whether you're 18 or 98, if technology feels confusing, we're here to help.

## 🎨 Visual Design Principles

Every design should answer these questions:

✅ Can I trust you?
✅ Are you patient?
✅ Can you really help me?
✅ Is this affordable?
✅ How do I get started?

### Visual Principles

- ✅ Rounded corners (warm, approachable)
- ✅ Warm illustrations with recurring characters
- ✅ Friendly typography
- ✅ Minimal borders
- ✅ Soft shadows (not hard, corporate shadows)
- ✅ No harsh colors
- ✅ No clutter—lots of breathing room
- ✅ Warm lighting in all illustrations
- ✅ Accessible contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)

### What We're NOT

❌ Corporate (Geek Squad, Best Buy aesthetic)  
❌ Sterile (clinical, cold, uninviting)  
❌ Stock photo (generic, impersonal)  
❌ Dark mode (cold, corporate)  
❌ Complex (confusing, cluttered)  
❌ Jargon-heavy (intimidating)  
❌ Fast-paced (we're patient)

## ✨ The Emotional Goal

This is something we rarely see businesses define, but it's critical for ours:

When someone visits your website or attends a workshop, they should feel:

- 🤗 **Welcome** — This is a place for me
- 🙋 **Understood** — They get my situation
- 😌 **Safe asking questions** — No judgment here
- 💪 **Encouraged** — I can do this

- 🌟 **Hopeful** — I can learn this

**That emotional experience is as important as the information itself.**

## 📱 Accessibility Standards

- **WCAG AA compliance** at minimum
- **Large text options** — Minimum 14px default, up to 18px+
- **Keyboard navigation** — Everything works without a mouse
- **Semantic HTML** — Proper heading structure, labels, etc.
- **Color contrast** — 4.5:1 minimum for text
- **Bilingual support** — English and Spanish equally supported
- **Adaptive technology** — Works with screen readers and magnification
- **Simple language** — Grade 6 reading level maximum
- **Consistent navigation** — Predictable, familiar patterns

## 💡 Design Principles (Everything Checklist)

Before you approve any design, ask:

- ✅ Is it easy to read? (large text, high contrast)
- ✅ Would an 80-year-old understand it? (clear, jargon-free)
- ✅ Is it warm? (colors, tone, feeling)
- ✅ Does it reduce anxiety? (welcoming, safe)
- ✅ Is it welcoming? (friendly, inclusive)
- ✅ Does it build trust? (professional, consistent)
- ✅ Is it accessible? (WCAG AA, keyboard nav, screen readers)
- ✅ Is it bilingual-ready? (English and Spanish equal)
- ✅ Does it feel local? (community-focused, not corporate)
- ✅ Would our character recognize it? (matches our brand)

If the answer isn't yes, redesign it.
