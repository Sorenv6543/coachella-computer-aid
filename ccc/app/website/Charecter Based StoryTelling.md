One thing has become clear from everything we've worked on: your illustrations are becoming a brand asset, not just decorative images.

Rather than generating each image ad hoc, I'd recommend we establish a consistent illustration guide. That means every future image follows the same visual language:

The same warm color palette.
The same soft, rounded illustration style.
The same recurring characters (the friendly senior couple and others).
The same Coachella Valley feel, with San Jacinto Mountains, desert landscaping, and bright blue skies.
The same approachable expressions and accessible, uncluttered compositions.

Over time, this creates a recognizable identity that people associate with your organization.

I have one idea that I think would take the project to the next level

Instead of creating illustrations only as we need them, let's build a complete illustration library—around 75–100 coordinated illustrations before we finish the website.

That would give us everything we need for the homepage, service pages, workshops, guides, forms, blog articles, social media, flyers, presentations, and future mobile apps, all with a unified visual identity.
