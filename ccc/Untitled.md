Deliverables
1. Brand Bible

A polished PDF that defines your mission, visual identity, tone, colors, typography, illustration rules, accessibility standards, and writing style.

2. Vuetify Design System

A reusable UI kit built specifically for your stack (Vue 3 + Vite + Vuetify), including buttons, cards, forms, navigation, spacing, and themes.

3. Illustration Guide

A document showing every recurring character, color, scene type, and illustration rule so every future image stays consistent.

A professional blueprint for the homepage showing every section, interaction, and responsive layout before we write any code.

5. Asset Library

Organized folders for illustrations, icons, backgrounds, and UI assets so the project stays maintainable as it grows.

Our mission is to empower people throughout the Coachella Valley by making technology accessible, understandable, and less intimidating. The Why

This is something I think belongs in the Brand Bible, even if it never appears publicly.

Technology has become essential for everyday life—staying connected with family, accessing healthcare, applying for jobs, managing finances, and participating in the community. Yet many people are left behind because technology can feel overwhelming or inaccessible. We believe no one should be excluded simply because they need a patient teacher.

Who We Serve

Instead of listing demographics, I'd present them as people with stories.

Seniors

Helping them stay connected with family, avoid scams, and use technology confidently.

People with Disabilities

Making technology more accessible through adaptive tools, accessibility features, and personalized support.

Spanish-speaking Families

Offering bilingual assistance with devices, online services, school portals, healthcare websites, and government resources.

Veterans

Helping navigate VA portals, telehealth appointments, and online benefits.

Low-Income Families

Providing affordable technology support and connecting people with low-cost internet and device programs whenever possible.

Anyone Feeling Overwhelmed by Technology

Whether you're 18 or 98, if technology feels confusing, we're here to help.
